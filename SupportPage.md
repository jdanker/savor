# Savor — Support

Savor is a personal restaurant tracking app for iOS. Save places you want to try, mark the ones you've been to, and organize them into lists.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add a restaurant?**
Tap the **+** button and search for a restaurant by name. Select it from the suggestions to add it to your list.

**Can I edit or delete a restaurant I've saved?**
Yes. Swipe left on a restaurant to delete it, or swipe right to mark it as Been.

**How do lists work?**
Open the **Lists** tab to create your own lists. Press and hold any restaurant to add it to a list. Tap **Smart Sort** (the wand) to file your spots into Coffee, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Takeout automatically.

**Why does Savor ask for my location?**
Only to sort your saved restaurants by distance. Savor asks when you choose **Distance** sorting, uses your location on your device, and never stores or sends it. Everything else works if you decline.

**My restaurant search isn't returning results. What should I try?**
Check that your device has an active internet connection. Restaurant search requires network access to query Google Places.

**Does Savor back up my data?**
Savor stores your list locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, your app data is included in those device backups.

**Does Savor share my data with anyone?**
No. Savor has no accounts or servers, and your lists, visit history, and location stay on your device. Restaurant search and details come from Google Places; the full [Privacy Policy](/savor/privacy) explains exactly what is sent.
