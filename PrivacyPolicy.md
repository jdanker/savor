# Privacy Policy — Savor

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026
**Developer:** Jahred Danker

---

## Overview

Savor is a personal restaurant tracking app. It stores your data on your device and does not transmit it to any server controlled by the developer. This policy explains exactly what data is handled, by whom, and how.

---

## Data You Provide

When you add a restaurant to Savor, the following information is saved **locally on your device only**:

- Restaurant details from Google Places (name, type, price level, public rating, summary, website, and map coordinates)
- The date you added it
- Your visit status (been / not yet)
- The lists you create and which restaurants are in them

This data is written to files in your device's private app storage (`Documents/restaurants.json` and `Documents/lists.json`). It is never uploaded, synced, or shared with the developer or any third party.

---

## Location

Savor can sort your saved restaurants by distance from you. If you choose **Distance** sorting, Savor asks for permission to use your location while the app is open.

- Your location is used **only on your device**, to calculate how far each saved restaurant is from you.
- It is **never stored**, and never sent to the developer, Google, or anyone else.
- Savor only asks when you choose Distance sorting. You can decline or turn it off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Savor**; every other feature keeps working.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

Savor does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, or any account information
- Your location (it is used on your device only; see Location above)
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs (IDFA)
- Usage analytics or behavioral data
- Crash reports or diagnostic data

There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no backend servers associated with this app.

---

## Third-Party Services — Google Places

Savor uses the **Google Places API** to look up restaurant information. These requests are the only data that leaves your device.

**What is sent to Google:**
- Your search text when you search for a restaurant to add (e.g., "pizza downtown")
- Requests for details and photos of a restaurant you select
- Requests that refresh the details, photos, or map coordinates of restaurants you have saved, identified by Google's place ID

**What is not sent:**
- Your location
- Your lists, visit status, or the dates you added restaurants

Google's handling of this data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Savor does not receive or store any data Google collects as part of these requests.

---

## Local Storage and Caching

Savor uses three local storage locations, all managed entirely on your device:

| Location | Contents | Cleared when |
|---|---|---|
| `Documents/restaurants.json` | Your saved restaurants | You delete the app |
| `Documents/lists.json` | Your lists | You delete the app |
| `Library/Caches/PlacePhotos/` | Restaurant photos fetched from Google Places | You delete the app, or automatically by iOS under storage pressure |

None of these locations is accessible to the developer or any third party.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

All data Savor stores lives on your device. To permanently delete all app data, delete Savor from your device. No data is retained by the developer after deletion because no data was ever transmitted to the developer.
